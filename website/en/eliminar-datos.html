Delete your data or your account

VantixGym · Updated 2026-10-07 · Español

VantixGym (a trade name operated by Daniel Oliva) lets you delete your data without asking anyone. You can remove the whole account or just part of what you logged, from inside the app.

Delete part of your data, without closing the account

Everything you log can be deleted on its own from the app, and the deletion is immediate and permanent on our servers:

Meals: Food tab, tap the meal and choose Delete.
Progress photos: Progress tab, open the check-in and choose Delete; that check-in's photos go with it.
Photos or clips on your own exercises: open the exercise, Edit, then Remove next to the reference.
Workouts: Progress tab, History, open the session and choose Delete.
Weight, steps, water and supplements: each entry is deleted from its own card.
Coach conversation: Settings, Coach, Clear the conversation.

If you would rather we did it, write to soporte@vantixgym.app from your account's email saying what you want removed. We answer within 30 calendar days at the latest.

Delete your whole account

In the app: Settings → Delete account. You can also ask by writing to soporte@vantixgym.app from your account's email.

When you ask, the account is signed out on every device and becomes inaccessible straight away, and any subscription is cancelled. For 30 days you can get it back by signing in again; after that everything is erased for good, with no way back.

What is deleted

Your name, email and password; your workouts, routines and custom exercises with their photos or clips; your meals, diet plans and supplements; your weight, steps and water; your progress photos and coach notes; your conversations with the coach; your passkeys and your notification subscriptions.

What is kept, and for how long

Technical logs (IP address and access records): up to 30 days, for the security of the service.
Encrypted backups: up to 90 days; they overwrite themselves and are only read to restore the service after a failure.
Payment records: kept by Stripe or by the store you bought from (App Store or Google Play) for as long as tax and accounting law requires, normally five years. We keep only the transaction id, never your card details.
Meal photos and imported documents: never stored; they are analysed and discarded.

Contact

For anything about your data: soporte@vantixgym.app. More detail in our privacy policy.
