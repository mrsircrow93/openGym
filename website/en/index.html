Frequently asked questions

Do I need to download anything?

No. VantixGym works from the browser on any phone or computer. On iPhone and Android you can add it to the home screen and it behaves like an app.

Does the free trial ask for a card?

No. You create the account with email and password and get 7 full days. If you do not pick a plan at the end, your data is kept and you can come back any time.

Does the coach replace my nutritionist or trainer?

No, and it does not try to. If you have a nutritionist, the app follows their plan and the recipes respect it. The coach helps you stick to it and understand your numbers; your professional's instructions always come first.

What happens to my meal photos?

They are analysed to identify the foods and then discarded. They are not stored on our servers. What remains is the log: the foods and their values.

Can I cancel whenever I want?

Yes, from Settings → Subscription, without writing to anyone. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

What if I already use another training app?

You can import your history from Strong, Hevy or FitNotes, and your weight from Apple Health. Your years of progress are not lost.
